# LLM Usage Disclosure

## Statement

This paper was prepared with the assistance of AI writing tools (Claude, Anthropic). The AI was used for:

- **Drafting and editing:** Generating initial drafts of prose sections and iterative editing for clarity, conciseness, and style. The author directed all content decisions, structural choices, and argumentation.
- **Code generation:** Writing experimental scripts, analysis code, and figure generation scripts. All generated code was reviewed, tested, and validated by the author before inclusion.
- **Literature search assistance:** Identifying relevant papers via Semantic Scholar, arXiv, and OpenAlex API queries. All retrieved references were independently verified against primary sources.

## Author Responsibility

All research ideas, experimental design, methodology, analysis, interpretations, and conclusions are the sole intellectual contribution of the author. The author takes full responsibility for the accuracy and originality of all content. The use of AI tools was limited to accelerating writing and coding tasks; no AI system contributed to the conceptual or scientific substance of this work.

## Verification

The following measures were taken to ensure the integrity of AI-assisted content:

- All citations were independently verified against CrossRef, Semantic Scholar, and OpenAlex APIs (zero fabricated references)
- All experimental results were produced by reproducible scripts with pinned seeds and are verifiable via the companion reproducibility package
- AI-generated prose was reviewed for accuracy and passed through automated AI pattern detection (17-category regex scanner) to ensure natural writing quality
- Statistical analyses were validated using standard methods (Shapiro-Wilk normality test with paired t-test or Wilcoxon signed-rank as appropriate)

## Compliance

This disclosure is provided proactively in the interest of transparency and in alignment with evolving venue policies on AI writing assistance:

- **NeurIPS LLM Policy:** NeurIPS permits the use of LLMs for writing assistance provided the author takes full responsibility for the content. This disclosure satisfies that requirement.
- **ICLR 2026 LLM Disclosure Requirements:** While this paper targets NeurIPS, we include this disclosure in recognition of the field-wide trend toward mandatory LLM transparency (ICLR 2026 mandates disclosure for all submissions).

The author affirms that all intellectual contributions in this paper are original and human-authored.
