### Why NeurIPS Qualifies as Major Professional Publication

NeurIPS is one of the top three machine learning conferences worldwide, alongside ICML and ICLR. It is peer-reviewed by domain experts through a rigorous double-blind review process, has an international scope with authors and reviewers from over 50 countries, and maintains an expert editorial board (the program committee) comprising leading researchers from academia and industry across all major AI research institutions globally. NeurIPS proceedings are indexed by all major academic databases including Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, DBLP, ACM Digital Library, and IEEE Xplore.

With an acceptance rate of approximately 25-28%, NeurIPS selects fewer than one in four submitted papers, ensuring that accepted work meets a high standard of scientific rigor, novelty, and significance. The conference attracts over 16,000 attendees annually, making it one of the largest gatherings of AI/ML researchers and practitioners in the world.
